Lesson clash

Press the tutorial(s) that clash, say what they clash with, and the class_clash message goes to all three phones. Tapping Actions taken 已完成調堂 in WhatsApp — or the buttons here — marks a lesson arranged and tells everyone.

1 · What's the event?

Leave the times blank for a whole-day event. The header date is what shows in “Possible clash - Kingsley 22/8” — it defaults to today.

2 · What it clashes with

Make-ups go in the same week as the lesson and before the day it was missed, keeping the tutor's usual time. Untick anything that doesn't need arranging, or type a different date and time.
