Settings

Tutorials and phone numbers

The day and start time are what let a clash be spotted automatically, and what a make-up is scheduled around. A tutorial without them can still be picked by hand. Make-up slots are for a centre that only runs the subject at fixed hours — list them (Mon 6pm, Sat 2-4pm) and a make-up will only ever be put in one of those. Leave it blank for a tutor who moves to suit, and the lesson keeps its usual time. Tick home class for a lesson that could be given up if it is the only thing blocking a centre make-up — you will always be asked before anything is skipped. Online slots are the hours the same centre runs the subject online; they show up as an Online tick on the clash itself, and only count once you take it.

Numbers are stored server-side only. Unticking notify mutes someone without losing their number.
