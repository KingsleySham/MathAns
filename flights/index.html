7Caveats

  • Cathay Pacific–marketed flights only. Partner-marketed sectors earn on a different chart this tool does not model — it shows them as unknown, never a guessed number.
  • One eligible CX sector minimum in the membership year for any upgrade or renewal to count.
  • All fares in the planner are estimates until the tracker's observed prices replace them. Tracked quotes from the Amadeus test environment are cached, non-live fares and are badged as such.
  • Booking class and fare brand matter far more than cabin — the same ultra-short Economy seat earns 25 points in Y-Flex and 3 in Q-Light.
  • Where a tracked quote's fare brand is unknown, its points figure is an upper bound (assumes Flex) and is shown with a ≤ sign.
  • Points accrue per sector; this build prices nonstop returns only. Connecting routings (e.g. HKG–KHH–TPE–HKG) earn more and are deliberately out of scope for now.
